Compassionate Canine Care Tailored to Every Breed

Founded in Hamilton by passionate dog lovers, Snazzy Paws Grooming pairs clinical hygiene standards with gentle, positive-reinforcement coat care. Whether your puppy requires an introductory desensitisation bath, your senior companion needs supportive orthopaedic floor grooming, or your double-coated breed requires an intensive undercoat blowout, our qualified salon team delivers individualized grooming in a calm, crate-free environment.
